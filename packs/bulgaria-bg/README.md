# Bulgaria Construction Pack

Configures OpenConstructionERP for the Bulgarian construction market. A
Bulgarian estimate is a КСС (количествено-стойностна сметка), a priced bill
of quantities with all-in unit rates. Since 1 January 2026 it is priced in
euro, with ДДС (данък върху добавената стойност, VAT) added once, at the end.

## What makes a Bulgarian estimate Bulgarian

The designer issues a количествена сметка: items, units and quantities. The
contractor prices it into a КСС without changing any of them. Every line
carries an единична цена built in an анализ from labour, materials and plant,
with the contractor's допълнителни разходи, доставно-складови разходи and
печалба already inside the rate, so no overhead or profit lines follow. A
public tender asks for those percentages as the bidder's ценообразуващи
показатели for exactly that reason. The сметка closes with "Общо без ДДС",
"ДДС 20 %" and "Всичко с ДДС".

Every construction falls in one of six categories (ЗУТ чл. 137). The category
decides who approves the design and issues the permit, whether a licensed
consultant must supervise the works (categories I to IV), and how the works
are put into service: a разрешение за ползване from the ДНСК (I to III), an
удостоверение from the municipality (IV and V), or neither (VI).

Every firm is identified by its ЕИК, nine digits (thirteen for a branch) with
a check digit. A VAT-registered company's ДДС number is "BG" and its ЕИК.

## What this pack enables

- **Euro, with the lev at its fixed rate.** New projects are in EUR. A lev
  amount converts at 1.95583 lev per euro, divided and rounded half up to the
  cent the way ЗВЕРБ чл. 12 prescribes, everywhere the platform converts
  currency, and a new Bulgarian project starts with the lev at that rate in
  its own rate table. The Sofia cost database, published in lev, therefore
  prices a euro КСС correctly.
- **ДДС at 20 %.** A Bulgarian bill gets one ДДС line on the direct cost,
  because the unit rates already carry overheads and profit; the 9 % and 0 %
  rates are on file for the cases that need them. The pack's estimating
  methodology, `bulgaria`, is the same single line.
- **ЕИК in the company profile.** Settings, Company profile asks for the
  ЕИК, checks its check digit (and the branch digit of a thirteen-digit code)
  and prints it as "ЕИК …" on every letterhead.
- **ЗУТ category in project setup.** The project form and Project Settings
  offer "Категория по ЗУТ" I to VI for every project in Bulgaria, with what
  each category means for supervision and putting into service. Any other
  value is refused.
- **Bulgarian interface with the trade's own words.** The interface starts
  in Bulgarian, and an overlay (`locales/bg.json`) names the bill the one way
  an estimator does, КСС or количествено-стойностна сметка, where the shipped
  strings mixed "BOQ", "КСС" and "количествена сметка"; it also renames
  "Търгуване" (trading) to "Търгове". The overlay is generated by
  `tools/build_vocabulary.py`, which states its rules and can be rerun after
  an upstream merge.
- **Sofia cost database** (`BG_SOFIA`) preloaded from the CWICR catalogue.
- **A demo project**: a residential block in Sofia (Манастирски ливади), 42
  flats over an underground garage, ЗУТ category III, priced as an all-in КСС
  at Sofia 2026 levels in EUR excluding ДДС (about 723 EUR per m2 GFA).
- **Bulgarian rule set** (`bulgaria`), switched on for every new project and
  for every project in Bulgaria:
  - a КСС that names its количествена сметка in the bill header keeps every
    description, unit and quantity of it and adds no line (ЗОП);
  - ДДС is one line, at 20 % (ЗДДС чл. 66, ал. 1), charged on all of "Общо без
    ДДС" with nothing added after it; a bill with no ДДС line is a warning
    only, since a contractor below the registration threshold charges none.

  No DIN 276 rule set: a КСС carries no DIN 276 cost groups, and the cost
  group rules failed every line of one.
- **Five reference documents** covering the КСС, the ЗУТ categories, public
  procurement under the ЗОП, ДДС and the euro changeover.

## Standards referenced

- Закон за устройство на територията (ЗУТ), чл. 137
- Наредба № 1 от 30.07.2003 г. за номенклатурата на видовете строежи
- Наредба № 3 от 31.07.2003 г. за съставяне на актове и протоколи по време на строителството
- Закон за обществените поръчки (ЗОП)
- Закон за данък върху добавената стойност (ЗДДС), чл. 66
- Закон за въвеждане на еврото в Република България (ЗВЕРБ), чл. 12
- Council Regulation (EU) 2025/1409 and Council Decision (EU) 2025/1407
- Закон за регистър БУЛСТАТ, чл. 6

These are referenced for interoperability and compliance checking. Nothing in
this pack is legal, tax or regulatory advice.

## Install

This pack ships inside OpenConstructionERP. Activate it from Modules then
Partner Packs: click Rescan, find "Bulgaria Construction Pack", then Activate
pack.

To run a workspace that boots straight into it:

```bash
OE_PACK=bulgaria-bg openconstructionerp serve
```
